Privacy policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

Air Ledger is an asset, service and F-gas record app for air conditioning businesses. It is run by Altitude Electrical Ltd, company number 00000000, registered office address ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the Air Ledger website and phone apps hold, why, and what you can do about it.

Who controls the data

For your own account (your name, email address and sign-in), we are the data controller.

The records a business keeps in Air Ledger, such as its customers, sites, equipment, jobs, photos and invoices, belong to that business. The business is the controller of that information and we process it on its behalf, only to run the service. If you are a customer of a business that uses Air Ledger, please contact that business about your information.

What we collect

InformationWhy we hold it
Email address and password (stored only as a secure hash)To create your account and sign you in.
Business details you enter, such as business name, phone, address and F-gas certificate numberTo show them on labels, reports and invoices you produce.
Records you enter: customers, sites, equipment, jobs, materials, invoices and reportsThis is the service itself. It is stored so you can see it on any of your devices.
Photos you add to jobs and reportsTo keep them with the job record.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, we do not sell data, and we do not use it to profile anyone.

Phone permissions

  • Camera: used only when you scan an asset sticker or take a job photo. Scans are read on the phone; only photos you choose to add are uploaded.
  • Notifications: service-due reminders are worked out and scheduled on your phone. No reminder data is sent to us or anyone else.
  • Storage: a copy of your records is kept on the phone so the app works without signal. It is removed when you sign out.

Where it is stored

Records, sign-in details and photos are stored with our hosting provider Supabase, in its London (UK) region. The website is served by GitHub Pages. These providers act as our processors under written terms. Data is encrypted in transit, and access to each business's records is limited to that business's signed-in users.

How long we keep it

We keep your records for as long as your account is open. F-gas rules require businesses to keep leak-check records for at least five years, so please download a backup before closing your account if you need them. When an account is deleted, its records and photos are removed from our live systems straight away and from backups within 30 days.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and all its data from inside the app, in Settings. You can also email us at support@airledger.co.uk and we will do it for you within 30 days.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct, export or delete your personal information, or object to how it is used. Email support@airledger.co.uk. If you are unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

Air Ledger is a business tool and is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top, and tell account holders by email about any significant change.
