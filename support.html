Common questions

A sticker won't scan

Tap Scan, hold the phone 10 to 20 cm from the label and keep it steady. If the label is damaged, type the ID printed under the code (it starts with AC-).

I'm not getting service reminders

Check that notifications are allowed for Air Ledger in your phone's Settings. Reminders arrive at 8am on the day a system comes into its reminder window, and on the day it falls due. You can change the reminder window in Air Ledger Settings.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. The app shows your last synced records, and changes you make offline are sent automatically when you're back online. Photos need a connection to upload.

How do I export my records?

In Settings you can download a full backup, the F-gas register and Xero invoice files. On a phone these open the share sheet so you can email or save them.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings and choose Delete account. This removes your account and all its records. Download a backup first if you need to keep F-gas records. See the privacy policy for details.
