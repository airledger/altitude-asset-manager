Common questions

Missing something?

Tell us what you'd like Air Ledger to do at support@airledger.co.uk and we'll look at getting it built.

A label won't scan

Tap Scan, hold the phone 10 to 20 cm from the label and keep it steady. If the label is damaged, type the code printed under the QR code (it starts with AC-).

I'm not getting service reminders

Check that notifications are allowed for Air Ledger in your phone's Settings. Reminders arrive at 8am on the day an install comes into its reminder window, on the day it falls due, and when an F-gas leak check is due. Open the app now and then so it can plan them.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. The app shows your last synced records, and changes you make offline are sent automatically when you're back online. Photos and site notes wait on the phone and send when signal returns. Signing off a service report needs signal.

How do I export my records?

The asset register (Clients), parts orders, service reports and invoice exports for Xero, QuickBooks and Sage all download as files. On a phone these open the share sheet so you can email or save them.

How do I delete my account?

On Home, tap Your account, then Delete my account. A business that only you belong to is deleted with it. Download what you need first, because F-gas records must be kept for 5 years. See the privacy policy for details.
