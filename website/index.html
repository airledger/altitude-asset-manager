Built on the tools, not in a boardroom. Air Ledger started inside a working AC company, because the big software was too expensive and the cheap software didn't know what a refrigerant charge was.

Why switch

Three things your current setup doesn't do

Every unit, labelled and logged

Design your own branded QR labels in the app and print them. Scan any unit on site and its full history, photos and refrigerant details are right there.

F-gas done for you

Enter the gas and the charge. Air Ledger works out the CO₂ equivalent and leak check intervals, and keeps the records ready for an audit.

Jobs to invoices, no chasing

The engineer finishes the job, the customer signs the report, and the invoice drafts itself for Xero, QuickBooks or Sage. Engineers never see your prices.

How it works

From call-out to cash in four steps

  1. Book itAdd the job against the client and the unit. It lands in the engineer's calendar.
  2. Scan itOn site, scan the label. Everything about that unit is on the phone.
  3. Sign itPhotos, readings and the customer's signature go on a branded service report, emailed automatically.
  4. Bill itThe invoice is drafted from the job, at your contract or call-out rates, ready to send to your accounts.
Asset labels

Your name on every unit you look after

Other apps sell you a roll of plain QR stickers. Air Ledger lets you design labels with your logo, colours and phone number, then print them on tough, weatherproof stock.

When the next engineer, the facilities manager or the customer scans the unit, they see it's yours, and who to call.

Questions

Good questions

When can I start using it?

Paid plans open in January 2027. Join the waiting list and we'll email you the day it opens, with your founding member price held.

Can I bring my data from my old system?

Yes. Upload your clients, sites and units from a spreadsheet or an export from your current software, and we'll help you check it's all come across.

Do my engineers need a laptop?

No. Air Ledger works on any phone or tablet, and the office side works in any web browser.

Can engineers see what we charge?

No. Prices and invoices are only visible to owners and the office. Engineers see the job, the unit and the report.

No long contract. Pay monthly and cancel any time, or pay yearly and get 2 months free.
